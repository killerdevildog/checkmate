# Project Goal

Operation Checkmate is a citizen-authored proposal for a Utah statute intended to make government actors personally accountable for abuses of public power. It is drafted to take effect as ordinary legislation; no state constitutional amendment is known to be necessary for it to take effect. It seeks to apply constitutional and legal duties to officials, agencies, public bodies, jurors, and government contractors; remove immunity defenses for unlawful official conduct; define criminal and civil consequences for violations; and provide remedies for affected citizens.

The proposal also seeks to create citizen-led enforcement through Citizen Councils empowered to investigate alleged misconduct, obtain records, initiate prosecution procedures, seek warrants, and review convictions when existing institutions do not act. Its broader stated objective is to keep government authority subject to the Constitution and the same standards of truth, law, and accountability applied to private citizens.
