# Candidate "-ism" Offenses for Operation Checkmate

This is a working reference of candidate `-ism` offenses for the Act — patterns of government-actor corruption that could be defined as substantive offenses under Section 17, paralleling the existing **Terrorism** (§1.1.12), **Cabalism** (§1.1.6), and **Tribalism** (§17.10A) provisions.

Each entry below identifies a coherent corruption pattern that could be named as the *practice of* that conduct by a government actor under color of authority. For each, the entry includes:

- A one-line description of the corruption pattern the offense would reach
- A note on overlap with existing bill provisions (whether it would function as an umbrella offense, fill new ground, or be redundant)
- A preliminary classification recommendation

The principal author of the bill will review this list and indicate which candidates to add as substantive offenses. Until then, none of the candidates below have been added to the operative bill text.

---

## Already in the Act (for reference)

| Term | Bill Provision | What It Reaches |
|---|---|---|
| **Terrorism** | §1.1.12 | Threats or fear-imposition targeting a citizen's safety, financial, family, or business welfare |
| **Cabalism** | §1.1.6 | Coordinated action by two or more government actors against a citizen |
| **Tribalism** | §17.10A | Selective use of official power on tribal-identity grounds (political, factional, family, religious, ideological, racial, etc.) |

## High-priority candidates (genuine new ground or strong umbrella value)

### Power-concentration / authority-overreach

- **Authoritarianism** — practice of using public office to concentrate power beyond constitutional limits. *Umbrella over §1.1.10 (Anarchy), §1.1.18 (Ultra Vires), §17.24 (Emergency Power Abuse).* Recommended classification: Class 1 felony base, capital where sustained or coordinated.
- **Despotism** — practice of exercising arbitrary power without accountability or limit. *Adjacent to authoritarianism but distinct emphasis: arbitrariness rather than concentration.* Recommended classification: Class 1 felony base.
- **Caesarism** — practice of personalizing public office; treating public power as personal dominion. *Adjacent to §17.10 Abuse Under Color of Office; specific to the "office as personal property" dynamic.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Statism** — practice of expanding governmental reach for its own sake, beyond any legitimate public purpose. *Reaches the regulatory-expansion-for-expansion's-sake pattern §17.23 touches but doesn't name.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Praetorianism** — practice of using security forces (police, sheriff, federal agents, state guard) to intervene in political or civic disputes. *Distinct from existing offenses; reaches militarization-against-civilians.* Recommended classification: Class 1 felony base, capital where elections or rights-suppression involved.
- **Imperialism (internal)** — practice of extending an office's jurisdiction beyond its constitutional grant. *Reaches the agency-creep / mission-creep pattern.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.

### Self-enrichment / network-favoritism

- **Cronyism** — practice of favoring friends, business associates, or social-network members in official decisions. *Umbrella over §17.21 (Preferential Contracting Abuse) and §17.10A (Tribalism); distinct in capturing the friend-of-friend network.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Nepotism** — practice of favoring family members in official decisions. *Distinct from §17.10A Tribalism (family is one of several tribal-identity grounds); standalone offense would capture the specific family-corruption pattern.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base, Class 1 where pattern.
- **Clientelism** — practice of exchanging public favors (jobs, contracts, services, enforcement decisions) for political support, votes, or loyalty. *Vote-buying / favor-trading specifically; distinct from cronyism/nepotism axis.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base, capital where election-affecting.
- **Kleptocratism** — practice of operating public office as a personal or factional theft enterprise. *Umbrella over §1.1.7 (Government Racketeering), §17.9E (Revenue Extraction).* Recommended classification: Class 1 felony base.
- **Plutocratism** — practice of using public office to systematically favor wealthy interests over ordinary citizens. *Distinct enough from existing offenses to warrant separate naming; reaches donor-capture pattern.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base, capital where pattern.
- **Privilegism** — practice of using public office to create or maintain a privileged class of citizens with different access, treatment, or exemptions than ordinary citizens. *Reaches the "rules don't apply to us" mechanism the whole bill is built to address.* Recommended classification: Class 1 felony base.

### Group-targeting / discrimination

- **Sectarianism** — practice of favoring or disfavoring citizens on religious-faction grounds specifically. *Distinct from §17.10A Tribalism by being religion-specific; could be a tribalism sub-offense or standalone.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Classism** — practice of using public office to favor or disfavor citizens based on socioeconomic class. *Adjacent to §17.9E (Revenue Extraction) and §17.7B (Status-Based Survival Enforcement) but broader.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Nativism** — practice of using public office to favor native-born or in-group citizens against immigrants, naturalized citizens, or those perceived as outsiders. *Adjacent to §2.8.5 (Citizenship-Claim Detention Obstruction) but broader.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Communalism** — practice of officials systematically favoring their own ethnic, religious, or geographic community at the expense of others. *Specific community-favoritism beyond what tribalism captures.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.

### Process / enforcement-abuse

- **Pretextualism** — practice of using pretextual legal grounds (false stated reasons, manufactured technical violations, pretextual stops) to take official action that would not be authorized on the actual reason. *Reaches the* Whren v. United States *pattern directly; touched by §17.14 (Enforcement Without Articulable Basis) but a dedicated naming would strengthen.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Proceduralism (abusive)** — practice of using procedural rules to obstruct substantive justice. *Reaches the "form over substance" / "we lost your paperwork" pattern; adjacent to §17.9 (Failure of Transparency) and §17.4 (Deliberate Record Distortion).* Recommended classification: Class 3 felony base, Class 2 where pattern.
- **Obstructionism** — practice of using public office to obstruct legitimate citizen processes (complaints, FOIA, Council action, court access). *Umbrella over §1.1.8 (Obstruction), §17.9A (Complaint Intake Refusal), §2.10 series.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Formalism (abusive)** — practice of using technical formal compliance to defeat substantive rights or duties. *Captures the "we technically followed the procedure even though the procedure was a sham" defense.* Recommended classification: Class 3 felony base.

### Information / truth-manipulation

- **Propagandism** — practice of using public office or public communication channels to disseminate false, misleading, or one-sided information to citizens. *Reaches official misinformation; distinct from individual lying offenses by capturing the institutional / channel-control pattern.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Censorism** (or **Censorialism**) — practice of using public office to suppress, prohibit, demote, or punish speech. *Umbrella over §17.9G (Protected-Speech Suppression).* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base, capital where it targets political dissent or whistleblower speech.
- **Sophism** — practice of using clever but misleading argumentation in official capacity to deceive citizens, juries, or the public. *Reaches the "lawyered up to mislead" pattern more specifically than §17.6 (Public Trust Fraud).* Recommended classification: Class 3 felony base.
- **Revisionism (factual)** — practice of altering, suppressing, or rewriting official records, history, or public information to change the operative reality. *Adjacent to §17.4A (Public-Record Spoliation) but distinct in capturing systemic-narrative-altering pattern.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Cynicism (institutional)** — practice of institutional indifference, dismissiveness, or contempt toward citizens, complaints, or rights claims, normalized as office culture. *Reaches the "no one cares" institutional posture; distinct from individual misconduct.* Recommended classification: Class 3 felony base.

### Cultural / civic

- **Demagogism** (the *practice* of demagoguery) — practice of using public office and public communications to inflame base instincts, fear, or factional resentment for personal political gain. *Reaches the politician-stoking-mob pattern.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base, capital where it directly produces violence or rights-suppression.
- **Cultism (institutional)** — practice of using public office to protect or extend cult-like loyalty dynamics within an agency, department, or political organization. *Reaches the "blue wall," prosecutorial-office-loyalty, and legislative-caucus-omertà patterns under one umbrella.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Mandarinism** — practice of bureaucratic-class self-protection at the expense of citizens. *Reaches the institutional / professional-class self-dealing pattern distinct from individual corruption.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Oligarchism** — practice of small-group dominance over the operative decisions of an office, agency, or governing body. *Adjacent to §1.1.6 Cabalism but emphasizes structural rather than conspiratorial dynamics.* Recommended classification: Class 1 felony base.

## Mid-priority candidates (more specific, real but narrower coverage)

- **Bossism** — practice of "political boss" patronage; an old-school named pattern of urban political corruption (Tammany Hall, etc.). *Adjacent to Cronyism + Clientelism.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Caudillism** (or **Caudillismo**) — practice of strongman-style executive governance characterized by personal loyalty over institutional procedure. *Adjacent to Caesarism with stronger personalization emphasis.* Recommended classification: Class 1 felony base.
- **Bonapartism** — practice of populist authoritarian executive governance leveraging democratic plebiscite to extend power beyond constitutional limits. *Adjacent to Authoritarianism, with a specific historical analog.* Recommended classification: Class 1 felony base.
- **Juntaism** — practice of small-group seizure or control of an institution's operative decisions, especially through coordinated exclusion of legitimate participants. *Adjacent to Cabalism + Oligarchism.* Recommended classification: Class 1 felony base.
- **Predationism** — practice of using public office to target vulnerable citizens (homeless, elderly, disabled, indigent, immigrants) for enforcement or extraction. *Adjacent to §17.7B (Status-Based Survival Enforcement) and §17.9E (Revenue Extraction).* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base, capital where targeting elderly, disabled, or children.
- **Extractivism** — practice of using public office to extract economic value from citizens beyond any legitimate public purpose. *Substantial overlap with §1.1.7 (Government Racketeering); umbrella value is limited.* Recommended classification: Class 2 felony base.
- **Profiteering** (the *practice of profiteerism*) — practice of using public office to obtain personal or factional financial benefit from public crises, emergency procurement, or asymmetric information. *Distinct enough from existing offenses to warrant naming, particularly around emergency procurement and crisis exploitation.* Recommended classification: Class 1 felony base, capital where emergency-related.
- **Bureaucratism** — practice of administrative overreach for its own sake; bureaucratic-institutional self-justifying expansion. *Overlap with Statism and Mandarinism.* Recommended classification: Class 3 felony base.
- **Theatricalism (governmental)** — practice of performative governance (press conferences, photo ops, performative arrests, performative investigations) that substitutes appearance for substantive action. *Adjacent to §17.6 (Public Trust Fraud); distinct in capturing the appearance-over-substance dynamic.* Recommended classification: Class 3 felony base.
- **Tokenism** — practice of performative inclusion, representation, or rights-recognition that substitutes appearance for substantive accommodation or remedy. *Distinct from existing offenses; reaches the "we made a committee" pattern.* Recommended classification: Class 3 felony base.

## Lower-priority candidates (substantial overlap with existing provisions, or limited operative value)

- **Favoritism** (generic) — too vague to operate as a substantive offense on its own; subsumed by Cronyism, Nepotism, Tribalism, Clientelism, etc.
- **Sectionalism** — favoritism along sectional / geographic lines. Too situational to warrant standalone naming; reachable under Tribalism.
- **Boyarism** — old-Russian aristocratic favoritism. Too obscure as a named term; reachable under Privilegism or Tribalism.
- **Mob-ism / Gangsterism / Banditism** — government as gang. Too rhetorical; reachable under Kleptocratism + Cabalism.
- **Triumvirism** — three-person dominance. Too specific to triadic structure; reachable under Oligarchism or Cabalism.
- **Caciquism** — Spanish-colonial strongman local rule. Too obscure; reachable under Caesarism or Caudillism.
- **Surveillanceism** — abusive surveillance. Reachable under §17.24A (Algorithmic Authority Abuse) and existing privacy provisions.
- **Patronageism** — clunky; subsumed by Cronyism and Clientelism.
- **Anarchism** — the term has its own non-corruption usage; "Anarchy" in this Act has a specific defined meaning at §1.1.10 (government acting completely outside lawful authority).

## Politically loaded labels (NOT recommended for adoption)

The following are recognizable -isms but are loaded political labels rather than operative legal terms. Adopting them as offense names would draw partisan opposition out of proportion to the operative content. Recommend skipping.

- **Fascism** — loaded; substantive content reachable under Authoritarianism + Tribalism + Praetorianism
- **Communism** — loaded political label; not operative
- **Socialism** — political label; not operative
- **Capitalism / Anti-capitalism** — political label; not operative
- **Globalism / Nationalism** — political labels; specific operative content reachable under Nativism, Imperialism, Foreign-Funded Lobbying (§1.1.11)
- **Racism / Sexism / Homophobism / Transphobism / Ableism / Ageism** — these are real and the conduct they describe is real and harmful; the bill already reaches the underlying conduct through multiple specific provisions (§2.10 series, §17.9 series, §17.10A Tribalism, §1.1.3 Battery, etc.). Naming them as offenses by these labels would invite culture-war opposition. The same conduct is reachable under existing provisions without those labels.

## Highest-leverage focused recommendation

If the goal is to add a tight set of four to six new umbrella offenses with the strongest combination of distinct coverage, political durability, and operative clarity, the recommended set is:

1. **Cronyism** — fills the friend-network corruption gap
2. **Nepotism** — fills the family-corruption gap with a long-established named legal concept
3. **Sectarianism** — fills the religious-faction-favoritism gap (politically resonant across the spectrum)
4. **Pretextualism** — fills the *Whren v. United States* pattern with a dedicated named offense
5. **Authoritarianism** OR **Despotism** (pick one) — fills the power-concentration umbrella
6. **Propagandism** — fills the institutional-misinformation gap

Adding all six would give the Act a coherent expansion of named umbrella offenses while preserving the existing §17.10A Tribalism, §1.1.6 Cabalism, §1.1.12 Terrorism architecture.

A second tier worth considering after the focused six:

- **Praetorianism** — for the militarization-of-civilians pattern
- **Clientelism** — for the vote-buying / favor-trading pattern
- **Kleptocratism** — as an umbrella over the racketeering / extraction architecture
- **Censorism** — as an umbrella over the speech-suppression provisions
- **Profiteering** — for the crisis-procurement pattern

The remaining candidates are real but would more efficiently be reached as factual patterns within existing or new umbrella offenses rather than as standalone provisions.
