# Contacted

This is the public record of outreach concerning **Operation Checkmate** — every politician, journalist, commentator, agency, organization, or other entity that has been approached to read, comment on, support, criticize, or cover the bill. It exists for transparency: any citizen, journalist, or supporter can see at a glance who has been asked, what their response has been (if any), and what the current state of each conversation is.

The file is updated as outreach moves through its stages. If you have outreach you want recorded here — your own letter to a legislator, an op-ed pitched to a paper, an inquiry to an agency, a podcast invitation extended to a commentator — open a pull request or email **citizens.checkmate@gmail.com** with subject `CHECKMATE — contacted` and the entry will be added.

---

## Status flags

Each entity below carries one of the following status flags:

- **`draft`** — outreach has been drafted in this repository but **has not yet been sent**. The draft is linked in the entry and can be edited, reused, or sent by anyone.
- **`sent`** — outreach has been transmitted. No reply yet.
- **`acknowledged`** — recipient has confirmed receipt (auto-reply, brief acknowledgment, intake message). No substantive engagement yet.
- **`in conversation`** — back-and-forth is ongoing.
- **`responded — supportive`** — recipient has replied with a positive engagement (endorsement, agreement to read, commitment to cover, request for more information).
- **`responded — feedback`** — recipient has replied with substantive comments, edits, criticism, or questions. Detail in the entry.
- **`responded — declined`** — recipient has replied declining further engagement. Detail in the entry.
- **`no response`** — outreach was sent and a reasonable interval (typically 30 days) has elapsed with no reply.
- **`follow-up scheduled`** — a follow-up message or contact is planned for a stated date.
- **`closed`** — the matter has been concluded one way or another and no further action is contemplated.

A single entry may move through several flags over time. The full history of each entry is preserved below, with the most recent status displayed in the quick-reference table.

## How to add an entry

To record outreach to a person, agency, or organization not yet listed:

1. **Add a row to the Quick reference table** at the top.
2. **Create a section** under the appropriate heading (Politicians, Journalists and Commentators, Agencies and Organizations, or a new heading if a new category is warranted).
3. **Include**, at minimum:
   - **Role / title** — who they are and what they do.
   - **Why them** — one or two sentences on why this entity is being approached about this bill.
   - **Contact channel** — public business email, contact form, intake URL, mailing address, or whichever channel was used.
   - **Outreach draft** — link to the draft letter or email if one is in the repository.
   - **Current status** — one of the flags from the *Status flags* section above.
   - **First contact** — date of the first transmitted outreach, or `—` if still in draft.
   - **Last update** — date of the most recent status change.
4. **Add a History entry** for each status change, dated and with a brief one-paragraph description of what changed (sent, acknowledged, response received and its substance, follow-up scheduled, closed, etc.).

If you are sending outreach on behalf of Operation Checkmate as a citizen supporter, please record it here so the record reflects the full picture of who has been asked to engage.
